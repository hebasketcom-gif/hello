About Dr. Sana Afreen

Committed to Clinical Precision & Patient Comfort

Dr. Sana Afreen is a distinguished dentist who combines advanced clinical expertise with a deeply compassionate approach to oral healthcare. With a relentless focus on gentle treatment and state-of-the-art procedures, she ensures every patient achieves a healthy, radiant smile.

Her practice philosophy centers on comprehensive diagnostic care, patient education, and painless dentistry. Whether performing intricate root canal therapy, aesthetic smile makeovers, or routine preventive care, Dr. Sana Afreen maintains the highest international standards of safety and hygiene.
